Research profile

A practical neuroinformatics focus.

My current work centers on the parts of neuroscience that become valuable when they are made structured, reproducible, and computationally useful: literature evidence, morphology metadata, SWC files, and models.

01

Literature mining and data provenance

Reviewing publications for neuronal and glial reconstructions, tracing systems, figures, supplementary material, data links, and confidence levels for database inclusion.

02

Neuronal morphology and SWC workflows

Building fluency in morphology-focused datasets, bifurcating reconstructions, metadata quality, and the structure of digital neuronal traces.

03

Computational neuron modeling

Using Hodgkin-Huxley style simulations and NEURON-oriented coursework to connect biophysical model assumptions with measurable spike behavior.

Featured work

Hodgkin-Huxley Neuron Simulator

Built a biophysical neuron simulator using the Hodgkin-Huxley mathematical framework, investigated how sodium and potassium conductance parameters shape spike behavior, and validated outputs against NEURON for numerical and biological realism.
