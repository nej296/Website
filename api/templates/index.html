Computational Neuroscience

Neuroinformatics, morphology, and neuron modeling

I am focused on using computers, data curation, and mathematical models to study neurons. My work and projects emphasize digitally reconstructed neuronal morphologies, scientific literature review, public neuroscience datasets, and NEURON-based simulation workflows.

My planned senior project, Morphological Determinants of Neuronal Excitability, is designed to connect NeuroMorpho.org reconstructions, Allen Institute morphology and electrophysiology data, and NEURON simulations to study how neuronal structure relates to excitability.
