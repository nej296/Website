About

I am a computational neuroscientist working at the intersection of neuroinformatics, neuronal morphology, and biophysical simulation, curating and repairing digital neuron reconstructions for NeuroMorpho.Org while building validated models of membrane dynamics. My developing senior project uses Python and NEURON to integrate public morphology and cell-type data into a network model of the hippocampus.
