Research Assistant → Research Intern

June 2026—Present Krasnow Institute for Advanced Study
01

Literature mining

Identify neuronal and glial reconstructions in the literature using tracing-system evidence from Neurolucida, Imaris, Amira, Vaa3D, Simple Neurite Tracer, and CATMAID.

02

Data and metadata review

Verify reconstruction counts, figure evidence, supplementary files, data availability, DOI and PMID references, and downloadable records across GitHub, Dryad, Figshare, Zenodo, and Dataverse.

03

Morphology editing

Convert lab-submitted files to standardized SWC, then repair somas, broken segments, long connections, topology errors, and neurite labels using Fiji, Neuromantic, CVAPP, and NeuTube.

04

Quality control

Run each reconstruction through automated checks, resolve every flagged structural issue, and repeat validation until the morphology is clean and ready for publication in the database.
