Literature and data review

Identify neuronal and glial reconstructions using tracing-system evidence from Neurolucida, Imaris, Amira, Vaa3D, Simple Neurite Tracer, and CATMAID.

Verify article details, reconstruction counts, figures, supplementary files, data availability, DOI and PMID references, and records in GitHub, Dryad, Figshare, Zenodo, and Dataverse.

Morphology preparation

Convert files submitted by laboratories worldwide into standardized SWC using automated processing, Fiji, and Neuromantic.

Use CVAPP to mark and reposition somas, reconnect segments, repair topology and long connections, and label apical dendrites, basal dendrites, and axons before final quality control.
